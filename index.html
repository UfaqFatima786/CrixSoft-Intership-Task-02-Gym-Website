Facilities

Everything the work requires

Four zones, one membership. Move between them as your program demands — nothing is locked behind a separate pass.

Free Weight Floor

Full racks of bumper plates, 12 power cages, and a strongman corner for logs, sleds, and yokes.

Conditioning Bay

Rowers, bikes, sleds, and a turf lane for sprint work and carries — built for intervals, not idle cardio.

Recovery Room

Mobility mats, percussion tools, sauna, and a stretch bay with a coach on hand three days a week.

Studio Space

Mirrored studio for group classes — HYROX prep, mobility flow, and boxing conditioning on rotation.

Locker & Wash

Full lockers, hot showers, and a laundry drop for towels and loaner gear — in and out without the hassle.

Members' Kitchen

Fridge stocked for prepped meals, a blender bar, and bulk protein at cost — no vending machines.
